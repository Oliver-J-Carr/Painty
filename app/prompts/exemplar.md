# The exemplar sheets

Before the person's request, their first message shows you {{SHEETS}} exemplar sheets: practice pages, each {{SHEET_WIDTH}} x {{SHEET_HEIGHT}} pixels, painted with this same paint system. They are not your canvas and not the subject. They are there to show you exactly where shapes land for the numbers you write.

- Each numbered example on a sheet was painted by exactly the shapes listed under its number in the message.
- The pink marks are labels, not paint: a dashed outline is a shape's box, and a pink dot is a point you wrote (a box's corners, a line's ends, a bent line's middle, a trail's start and corners), with its coordinates beside it.
- Each sheet is {{GRID_NOTE}}.
- Study them before you plan: how a shape fills its box, where a rotated or skewed shape pokes outside its box, how far a bend pulls a line, and where trail legs land for each heading. Then plan your own shapes the same way, in your own canvas's pixels.
