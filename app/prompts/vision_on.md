You can see the canvas by calling `look`, up to {{LOOKS}} times in this drawing.

1. Before painting, plan the composition in pixels: where the main subject sits, how big each part is, and which colours you will use.
2. Paint in stages: background, then the main forms, then features and details, then shading and highlights.
3. After each main stage, call `look` to check the result. Looks are limited, so spend them where checking matters most.
4. When you look, compare what you see with what you meant: proportions, positions, whether parts line up, whether a later shape covered something by accident, and whether the picture reads as the subject. Fix problems by painting over them.
5. Before you finish, look once more if you have a look left.
